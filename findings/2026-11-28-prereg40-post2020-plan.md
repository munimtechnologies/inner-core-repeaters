## Question

Wang, Vidale et al. (2024, Nature, [10.1038/s41586-024-07536-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07536-4))
read South Sandwich repeaters as an inner core that turned around about 2008 and has since retraced its path
about 2.5 times more slowly. Their catalogue ends in January 2023. The multidecadal oscillation of Yang & Song
(2023) and Wu, Song & Yang (2026, GRL, [10.1029/2025GL119295](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL119295); travel times
at COLA to 2024) also has the inner core moving relative to the mantle after 2009. Zhang & Wen (2026, JGR,
[10.1029/2026JB034595](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026JB034595)) instead report episodic change of the inner-core
surface, with quiescence and recovery since 2022 in one family (M03-24).

If the inner core is still moving relative to the mantle, then pairs of repeating earthquakes recorded at the
Yellowknife array (YKA) after 2020 should differ more in late PKIKP coda the further apart in time they are.
If the coda has gone quiet, they should not. Every one-turn clock with its turn before 2020 maps post-2020 times
linearly, so the rank test below gives the same answer for all of them (no turn date or speed is fitted).

The test uses only post-2020 YKA data. That starts after the digitiser firmware fix of 2020-04-08 (Ackerley
2026, SRL, [10.1785/0220260111](https://doi.org/10.1785/0220260111)) and includes the families' 2023-2026 events,
which no published inner-core study has used across families.

## Data

`results/h13-v1/h13_pairs_scored.csv` at commit `4c44a952299eb29882930de2373c1aa16133e0ef`. The job checks it
against git blob `3bc1eacff2ce851426e9112083f98ae58afd9722`. This is the table of pre-registration 13: YKA pairs
of 83 South Sandwich families found by our own ILAR clustering, each with the late-coda difference D_c310
(3-10 s after PKIKP), the full-window D, and a mantle-P source score S from up to 8 stations that never see the
core.

Common filters: array YKA; M03-24 excluded; source verified (S <= 1/3 at >= 2 stations, the pre-registration 13
cut); finite times and metric.

- **Set P (post-2020):** both events on or after 2020-04-08 (decimal year 2020.2678), and neither inside YKA's
  second timing-error era, 2021-12-01 to 2022-04-01 (both ends inclusive).
- **Set C (calibration):** both events in [1990.0, 2008.5), at most 2.65 years apart. This is a period of
  forward motion that both rotation camps accept. 2.65 years is 6.6 years / 2.5: under Wang et al.'s clock,
  P's widest time span (2020.27 to 2026.9) covers the same clock distance as C's widest pair.

## Statistic

rho = Spearman rank correlation of D_c310 with |t2 - t1| over the pairs of a set (average ranks for ties).
The spread comes from a whole-family bootstrap: 2,000 resamples of families with replacement, seed 0 for P and
seed 1 for C. f_pos is the share of resamples with rho > 0. p95 is the nearest-rank 95th percentile of the
finite resampled rho.

## Rule

- P is **testable** if it has >= 20 pairs, >= 4 families and >= 5 pairs at least 2.0 years apart.
- C is **calibrated** if it has >= 20 pairs, >= 4 families, rho_C > 0 and f_pos_C >= 0.95.
- **Headline:**
  1. P not testable: **untestable**.
  2. rho_P > 0 and f_pos_P >= 0.95: **still-changing**. The coda kept changing with time after 2020, so the
     inner core (or its surface) under these paths was not quiet.
  3. Otherwise, if C is not calibrated: **insensitive**. Our measure does not even resolve the agreed
     pre-2008 change at these time spans, so a flat P means nothing.
  4. Otherwise, if p95_P < rho_C: **quiet-since-2020**. After 2020 the coda changes with time apart clearly
     less than it did over the same clock distances before 2008. Continued motion at the rate Wang et al.
     report would need the core to have slowed, stopped or changed character after 2020, or the change to have
     become invisible at YKA.
  5. Otherwise: **unresolved**.
- **Report-only** (never changes the headline): the same rule with full-window D; with M03-24 included; a
  permutation p for P (event times shuffled within families, 2,000 times, seed 0) and the same for C; medians of
  D_c310 in time-apart bins 0-1, 1-2, 2-4 and 4-7 years.

## What each reading predicts

- Steady one-turn backtracking (Wang et al. 2024) or the multidecadal oscillation (Yang & Song 2023; Wu et al.
  2026): still-changing, unless the motion is too slow to see in six years.
- Episodic surface change with quiescence since 2022 (Zhang & Wen 2026), if it holds beyond M03-24:
  quiet-since-2020 or unresolved.
- A pipeline that cannot see the change: insensitive.

## Limits fixed now

- One array and one measure. The S cut controls source differences only as far as mantle P at 8 stations can.
- Pairs share events, and the bootstrap resamples whole families to respect that, not single pairs.
- The post-2020 span is six years. A slow enough motion passes as "quiet".
- Family membership comes from our own ILAR clustering (pre-registration 13). Families are not Wang et al.'s
  multiplets.
- The legacy YKA hardware before 2014 had different noise. C is used only as a sign-and-size check on rho, not
  for absolute D values.
