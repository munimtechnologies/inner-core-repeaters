## Why this addendum

Pre-registration 16 says: "As far as we know, no one has asked whether the backtracking clock (as
opposed to a steady one) holds on these paths, or used events from 2019-2026 on them." A further
literature search on 2026-11-19 found that this is still true, but that the pre-registration left
out a line of earlier work on **temporal change seen at antipodal stations**. Vidale et al.
(2024, *Nature* 631:340) list "antipodal core waveform changes" among the earlier evidence for
inner-core differential rotation. Our results note must credit it and say how P16 differs.

## How P16 differs, and what that means for reading its result

1. **Clock, not rate.** The earlier antipodal time studies fit a steady differential rotation from
   a doublet or two per path. P16 compares a turn-back clock (turn 2008.5, ratio 2.5) with plain
   time order across 20 repeating families and 1998-2026.
2. **Different paths.** The earlier paths are Tonga/Sulawesi to Algeria/Brazil. P16 uses South
   Sandwich Islands sources and stations 155-179.5 deg away in north-east Asia and the western
   Aleutians, whose inner-core boundary crossings addendum G maps.
3. **Different phase.** The earlier time shifts were in PKIIKP or its precursors, which sample
   the top ~100 km of the inner core. P16's primary measure D uses the PKIKP (DF) window, onset
   -0.3 to +5 s, aligned on PKPab. In PREM, PKIIKP arrives about 30 s after PKIKP at the antipode
   (Butler & Tsuboi 2010), so near 180 deg it lies outside that window. We have **not** checked
   where PKIIKP falls at the shorter distances in P16 (155-170 deg), and we change no window
   because of it.
4. **Reading.** If P16 grades **no-time-signal**, this is not evidence against the earlier
   antipodal PKIIKP shifts, which come from a different phase sampling a shallower layer. If it
   grades **B-supported** or **M-supported**, the results note will say whether this agrees with
   a steady ~0.05 deg/yr rotation, using only the S16-c exploratory fit. It will not claim
   P16 is the first antipodal test of inner-core change. It may claim, if the result supports it,
   that it is the first test of the **backtracking clock** on near-antipodal paths, and the first
   to use 2019-2026 events on them.
