## Bottom line

* **The objective pipeline re-finds Zhang & Wen's multiplet M03-24 exactly.** All six
  published events land in one multiplet (our Z004), together with three more
  (2014-12-09, 2022-10-15 and 2025-12-13). Calibration holds.
* **Before 2025 the patch looks like backtracking.** The 2003 event's PKIKP looks like
  the 2020-2024 events and unlike the 2008-2014 ones. That is what the 2024 *Nature*
  model predicts: 2020-2024 sit at "equivalent dates" of 2002-2004.
* **The pre-registered out-of-sample tests could not be graded.** Of the 46 2025-2026
  events near the patch, only one (2025-12-13, mb 5.0) has a PKIKP signal above the
  pre-set quality gate and joins a pre-turn partner.
* **That one new event goes the other way (exploratory, one event).** Its PKIKP is nearly
  identical to the 2011 event (D = 0.033) and clearly unlike the 2003 event (D = 0.167).
  The backtracking model predicts the reverse. This is not a test result. A pre-registered
  robustness and second-array check was filed before any of it was run
  ([`2026-11-17-m0324-check-prereg.md`](2026-11-17-m0324-check-prereg.md)).
* **College, Alaska single-station BC−DF (cola-v2) is too sparse to decide anything.**
  Only 27 of 2,166 catalogue events gave a usable measurement, one of them from 2025.

## 1. The M03-24 patch (icr-m0324-v1)

Run: 2026-11-17 18:47-18:59 UTC on the Dell, `m0324.py` 0.5.0 with the unchanged ILAR
pipeline. It used 338 USGS events of M ≥ 4.3 within 35 km of the M03-24 centroid
(59.04°S, 25.69°W) from 1991, plus the six published members. Of these, 290 were beamed
(ILAR data effectively start in 2000), giving 20 multiplets, 1,110 pairs and 40 pairs
that pass the quality cuts. The analysis step finished (exit 0). The job is marked
failed only because its final unit-test step named two test files that were not in the
job folder. `test_m0324` itself passed 8 of 8.

### Pre-registered tests

| Test | Forecast | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Z0: reproduce M03-24 | holds 55% | **holds**: 6 of 6 published members processed, all 6 in one multiplet (Z004, 9 members) |
| Z3: calibration | holds 75% | **holds**: median D 0.047 for pairs < 2 yr apart vs 0.257 for pairs > 10 yr apart under both models (6 and 6 pairs) |
| Z1: new-event best match (main test) | testable 40% | **untestable**: 0 discriminating 2025-26 events (≥ 2 were needed) |
| Z2: out-of-sample rank test | (needs ≥ 6 pairs, ≥ 3 cross-turn) | **untestable**: 6 pairs but only 1 crosses the turn |
| Z5: best (turn, ratio), exploratory | — | T = 2007.25, ratio 4.0, ρ = 0.62 vs ρ_M = 0.12 (40 pairs) |

Z2's numbers are reported but, as pre-registered, not interpreted: ρ_B = 0.26 (p = 0.62),
ρ_M = 0.54 (p = 0.27), and 0% of multiplet bootstraps favour B.

**In-sample rank test (computed by the code, not one of the pre-registered tests).** On
34 pre-2025 pairs (13 cross-turn, 6 multiplets): ρ_B = 0.70 (p = 5×10⁻⁶) vs ρ_M = 0.11
(p = 0.53), and 92.8% of multiplet bootstraps favour B. At this patch the objective
measure reproduces the in-sample backtracking pattern more strongly than on the whole
ILAR set, where P1 narrowly missed its bar at 88%.

**Why Z1/Z2 were untestable.** 2025-2026 is busy at the patch. All 23 events tried from
2025 and 8 of 9 from 2026 were beamed. But almost all are mb 4.5-4.9, and their PKIKP
beam SNR is 0.8-2.0, below the pre-set gate of 3. In multiplet Z000, the 2001-01-29
event (the backtracking forecast partner) has three new members: 2025-11-08, 2026-06-23
and 2026-08-04. All three fail the gate (DF SNR 0.78, 2.05 and 0.96), so their D values
are noise. The magnitude cut was not the limit. PKIKP signal strength at ILAR in the
1-2 Hz band was.

### Objective D vs the published by-eye grades (multiplet A of Wang et al. 2024)

M03-24 is multiplet A of Wang et al. (2024), location C02, events 718/734/742/848/854/859.
Their Supplementary Table 2 grades 15 pairs among these six at ILAR (IM: 0 similar,
1 somewhat, 2 different). Grade by grade:

* the six grade-0 pairs have mean D 0.062;
* the five grade-1 pairs, 0.094;
* the four grade-2 pairs, 0.149.

Spearman ρ(grade, D) = **0.67** (n = 15, p ≈ 0.007). That is in line with the 0.62 found
on 17 pairs across the whole ILAR set (v0.7.1). Two pairs graded "different" by eye are
the largest D in the family: 2003-2008 (D 0.160) and 2003-2011 (0.235). The 2003-2020 pair
was graded 1 by eye and has the smallest D of any pair with 2003 (0.038). (Grades quoted
from Wang et al. 2024, *Nature* 631:340, Supplementary Table 2, only for this comparison.)

around 2003-2008 that later recovered.

### The 2025-12-13 event (exploratory; one event)

USGS us6000runq, 2025-12-13 14:04 UTC, mb 5.0. It is the only 2025-2026 event at the patch
that passes every gate (reference-window cc ≥ 0.993 with all members; DF SNR 3.24 against a
gate of 3).

* **Backtracking forecast.** Its equivalent date is e = 2001.5. Of the gated pre-turn
  partners it should look most like 2003 (|Δe| 1.7 yr) and least like 2011 (|Δe| 5.9 yr).
* **Observed.** It looks most like **2011 (D 0.033)**, then 2024 (0.048), 2020 (0.067), and
  least like **2003 (D 0.167)**. Over all eight partners, including the four below the
  gate, ρ(D, Δt) = 0.48 and ρ(D, Δe) = 0.10.
* **Not a location effect.** The new event's outer-core reference window matches the
  2003 event slightly better (cc_ref 0.998, detection cc 0.998) than the 2011 event
  (0.994, 0.995). So it is at least as close to the 2003 source.
* **Fast.** Within 1.7 years (2024.2 to 2025.95) the event moved from 2003-like to
  2011-like. The D to 2003 rose from 0.052 to 0.167. Rigid rotation would have to run about
  three times its pre-2008 rate to do this.
* **Why it is not yet a finding:**
  * it is one event at modest SNR;
  * the element-by-element median (Ds, noisier and not a pre-registered metric) does not
    show the gap (0.337 to 2003 vs 0.400 to 2011);
  * no second array has looked.

  The pre-registered follow-up (`m0324_check.py`) resamples ILAR's elements, tries three
  other PKIKP windows and measures the same nine events at YKA in Canada.

If it holds up, M03-24 would show change (2003→2008), quiescence (2008-2014), return
(2020-2024) and a **new departure in 2024-2025**. That favours episodic, local change of the
inner-core surface (Zhang & Wen 2026) over a steady retrace of the path (Wang et al. 2024)
at this spot. Future events then make a sharp prediction: backtracking says 2026 events
here should look 2003-like; a new change episode says 2011-like.

Another anecdote from a different multiplet (Z019), also exploratory: 2025-03-26 vs
2022-08-12 has D = 0.150 at high SNR (6.1/6.6) after 2.6 years. The calibration median for
pairs under 2 years apart is 0.047. Its reference correlation is lower (0.974).

## 2. College, Alaska single-station BC−DF (icr-cola-v2)

Run: 2026-11-17 18:16-18:33 UTC on Tango, `cola.py` 0.5.0. Catalogue: 2,166 SSI events
(M ≥ 5.0 before 2024-07, M ≥ 4.7 after), 1982-2026, at 148-156° from College.

* **Measured:** 1,022 events; 81 passed the quality flags; **27 usable** after the
  cycle-skip screen. By year: 1-3 per year, 0 in 2024 and 2026, **1 in 2025**. By channel:
  IU.COLA.00 14 of 1,618, DW.COL 8 of 320, IU.COL 5 of 161. Of the 320 DW.COL records,
  178 could not be cached ("DWWSSN" encoding); they were still measured in memory.
* **Phase timing check (as planned):** the median DF onset is 3.3 s before ak135 both
  before (n 22) and after (n 5) 2018-09-25. The median BC−DF residual is +3.1 s (p10-p90
  2.3-3.5 s). This is the known SSI-Alaska anomaly; the v0.5.0 search windows handled it.
* **C1 (calibration, recover the pre-2008 rise):** untestable (n = 16).
* **Q1 (in sample):** Bk fits best, but barely (RSS 4.84 vs L 4.91, P 4.87; n = 26).
  **Q3:** best turn 2005.0, ratio 1.0.
* **Q2 (out of sample):** testable but **not decisive** with one new event, for every model
  pair and in both bands.
* **Unit test.** The run's own unit test `test_cola.TestTrace.test_process_and_measure`
  failed. It processes a clean synthetic trace and expects it to be marked usable, but it
  was not. The test was written before the v0.5.0 window change and was not updated, so
  it may be stale. It may instead point to a real gate problem that also explains the
  low yield. This is unresolved and recorded here. No result above depends on it,
  because nothing above is decisive.

**Verdict.** Single-station BC−DF at College cannot decide the 2025-2026 question at this
quality gate. This lane is closed until the cause of the test failure is found. Wu, Song &
Yang (2026) used hand-picked, higher-magnitude records. Our objective picker keeps few.

## 3. What changed in the plan

* The M03-24 anomaly is now the lead thread. It gets a pre-registered check: element
  bootstrap, alternative windows, and the YKA array.
* The whole-arc forecast-date scan (`fdscan.py`, queued) now carries a sharper question:
  do other 2025-2026 events also break the backtracking forecast, or is M03-24 alone?
* The COLA lane is paused.
